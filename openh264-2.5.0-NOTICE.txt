Vi sao can tep nay
------------------

H.264 (fourcc "avc1"). Khong co tep nay:
  - FFmpeg in log do "Failed to load OpenH264 library"
  - VideoWriter fourcc "avc1" that bai -> video ket qua co the hong
  - Tien trinh co the CRASH luc thoat (0xC0000005)
Chi khi co encoder H.264 thi video .mp4 phat duoc tren trinh duyet
(Chrome/Edge/Firefox) va khong can dung codec du phong MPEG-4 Part 2.
